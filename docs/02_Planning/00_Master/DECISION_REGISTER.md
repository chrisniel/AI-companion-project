## 1. Architectural Freeze Policy

- **PC V1 Architecture is FROZEN:** No new PC V1 architecture topics may be introduced unless ongoing implementation or empirical benchmarking uncovers an unresolvable technical blocker, active security vulnerability, direct contradiction, or missing prerequisite.
- **Triage Destination for New Proposals:** Any emergent concepts, non-critical optimizations, or feature expansions outside the frozen baseline are routed strictly to [`DECISION_DEBT.md`](DECISION_DEBT.md), [`BACKLOG.md`](BACKLOG.md), or post-V1 release tracks.

## 5. Change Control & Reopening Threshold

To modify or reopen any decision in this register, a contributor or agent must demonstrate:
1. An empirical runtime or hardware incompatibility on supported platforms;
2. An insurmountable security or privacy vulnerability;
3. A verified internal contradiction between two locked decisions; or
4. A missing prerequisite discovered during implementation that blocks delivery.

Emergent ideas or convenience refactors are routed to [`DECISION_DEBT.md`](DECISION_DEBT.md) or [`BACKLOG.md`](BACKLOG.md).
